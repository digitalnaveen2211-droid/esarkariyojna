Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

Category: Kisan  |  Shuruat: February 2019  |  Vibhaag: Krishi aur Kisan Kalyan Vibhaag, Bharat Sarkar

Yojna kya hai?

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi, jise PM-KISAN ke naam se bhi jaana jaata hai, Bharat sarkar ki ek kendriya yojna hai. Iska makhsad chhote aur seemant kisan parivaaron ko unki kheti ki zaroorat ke liye ek nishchit aarthik sahayata dena hai, taaki beej, khaad, dawai aur ghar ke rozmarra ke kharche mein unhe madad mile. Ye yojna February 2019 mein shuru hui thi.

Is yojna ki sabse khaas baat ye hai ki paisa seedhe kisan ke bank khate mein jaata hai, beech mein koi bichauliya nahi hota. Ise Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) kehte hain. Isse madhyasthon ki bhoomika khatam hoti hai aur raashi sahi vyakti tak pahunchti hai.

Kitni raashi milti hai?

Patr kisan parivaar ko har saal kul ₹6,000 diye jaate hain. Ye raashi teen kisto mein milti hai, aur har kisht ₹2,000 ki hoti hai. Kisht ka samay aam taur par saal ke alag-alag mahinon mein hota hai. Har kisht ki tarikh sarkar ke official portal par ghoshit ki jaati hai, isliye samay-samay par pmkisan.gov.in dekhte rehna achha hai.

Dhyan dein ki ye raashi ek saath nahi di jaati, balki har kisht tab aati hai jab uski prakriya poori ho jaati hai. Agar kisi kisht mein deri hoti hai, to uska karan aksar bank khata, Aadhaar seeding ya e-KYC se judi koi kami hoti hai.

Kaun laabh le sakta hai? (Patrata)

Is yojna ka laabh un kisan parivaaron ko milta hai jinke naam par khetiyogya zameen hai. Parivaar ka matlab aam taur par pati, patni aur naabaalig bachche hote hain. Chhote aur seemant kisan, jinke paas aam taur par 2 hectare tak ki zameen hai, iske mukhya laabharthi hain.

Zameen ki maalik hona zaroori hai. Kiraye par kheti karne wale kisan, jinke naam par zameen record mein nahi hai, is yojna ke tahat seedha aavedan nahi kar sakte. Aise mamle mein sthaneey rajasv vibhaag ya krishi vibhaag se sampark karna upyogi hota hai.

Kaun patra nahi hai? (Apavaad)

Sarkari niyamon ke anusaar kuch shreniyan is yojna se bahar rakhi gayi hain. Ye suchi samay ke saath badal sakti hai, isliye final faisla official site par di gayi shart ke hisaab se hi maanen. Aam taur par ye shreniyan patr nahi maani jaati:

  • Sansthagat zameen-malik, jaise company ya sansthan jinke naam par zameen hai.
  • Sanvidhanik padon par kaarya kar rahe ya rah chuke vyakti (jaise mantri, saansad, vidhayak, purv mantri).
  • Kendra ya rajya sarkar ke sabhi niyamit karmchari, aur sarkari pension paane wale retired karmchari (kuch shreniyon ko chhod kar).
  • Aayakar daata (income tax payer) parivaar, kuch shart ke saath.
  • Doctor, engineer, vakil, CA aur aise peshewar jo apni professional income se jude hain, unki bhi kuch shreniyan.

Agar aap in shreniyon mein aate hain, to bhi ek baar official site par apni eligibility check karein, kyunki niyam ki bariki samay-samay par badal sakti hai.

Aavedan ke liye zaroori dastavez

Aavedan se pehle ye cheezein taiyar rakhein, taaki form bharte samay rukna na pade:

  • Aadhaar card, jo mobile number aur bank khate se linked ho.
  • Zameen ke kagaz, jaise khatauni ya khasra ki jaankari (rajya ke hisaab se naam alag ho sakta hai).
  • Bank khate ki jaankari, jisme IFSC code aur khata number sahi ho.
  • Sakriya mobile number jo Aadhaar se linked ho, taaki OTP aa sake.

Aavedan kaise karein? (saamanya prakriya)

Aavedan ka tareeka samay ke saath official portal par badal sakta hai, isliye neeche diye steps ek saamanya roop hain. Final prakriya official website par hi dekhein.

  1. Official website pmkisan.gov.in par jaayein.
  2. "Farmers Corner" ya "New Farmer Registration" option chunein.
  3. Aadhaar number, mobile number aur rajya ki jaankari bharein.
  4. Zameen ka record aur bank ki jaankari bharein. Kai baar zameen ka record apne aap check hota hai.
  5. OTP se verify karke form submit karein aur acknowledgement ya registration number save karein.

Registration ke baad bhi e-KYC zaroori ho sakta hai. Ise official portal ya PM-KISAN app ke zariye poora karein. Bina e-KYC ke kisht rukne ki sambhavna rehti hai.

Status kaise dekhein?

Apna registration status, kisht ki jaankari aur beneficiary status official portal par "Know Your Status" ya "Beneficiary Status" section se dekha ja sakta hai. Aadhaar, mobile ya registration number se search karke apni details check kar sakte hain. Agar koi galti dikhe, to sthaneey krishi karyalaya se sampark karein.

Aam galtiyan jo se bachein

  • Aadhaar aur bank khate ka naam alag hona, jisse raashi atak sakti hai.
  • Aadhaar se mobile number linked na hona, jisse OTP nahi aata.
  • e-KYC adhoora rehna.
  • Kisi bhi "fee" maangne wale dalal ya website se bachna. Is yojna mein registration ke liye paisa nahi lagta.
  • WhatsApp ya SMS par aaye kisi link par Aadhaar, OTP ya bank detail na dena.

Madad aur sampark

Sahi jaankari ke liye sabse achha tareeka hai official portal par diye gaye helpline number aur grievance section ka upyog karna. Sthaneey krishi vibhaag ya Common Service Centre (CSC) bhi aavedan mein madad kar sakte hain. Helpline numbers hamesha official site se hi check karein, kyunki unhe kisi third-party site par bharosa nahi karna chahiye.
